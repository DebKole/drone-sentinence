To get your drone flying perfectly autonomously indoors using Optical Flow and the Laser Rangefinder, here is the master checklist of all the parameters you need to set in Mission Planner.

I have grouped them by category so you can easily go down the **Config > Full Parameter List** tab and verify each one.

### 1. EKF3 (The Brain) & Sensors

These settings tell the drone's brain to completely ignore GPS and the Barometer, and trust *only* your Optical Flow and Rangefinder.

* **`AHRS_EKF_TYPE` = 3** (Tells the drone to use the modern EKF3 algorithm, which is best for non-GPS flight).
* **`EK3_SRC1_POSXY` = 0** (None. Tells it not to look for a GPS for horizontal position).
* **`EK3_SRC1_VELXY` = 2** (OpticalFlow. Tells it to use the camera for horizontal speed/movement).
* **`EK3_SRC1_POSZ` = 2** (RangeFinder. **Crucial:** This tells the drone to completely ignore the Barometer and use the Laser Rangefinder for its height. This stops the bouncy altitude).
* **`EK3_SRC1_VELZ` = 0** (None).

### 2. Compass Settings (To stop Gyro Creep / Diagonal Twist)

Since we want the drone to hold a perfectly straight heading without you having to manually correct it with the RC sticks, we need the compass ON to provide an absolute "North" anchor.

* **`COMPASS_USE` = 1** (Enables the primary compass).
* **`COMPASS_USE2` = 1** (Enables the secondary compass, if your hardware has one).
* **`COMPASS_USE3` = 1** (Enables the tertiary compass, if your hardware has one).

### 3. GPS & Arming Safety Checks

Because you don't have a GPS, the Pixhawk will refuse to arm unless you turn off the GPS safety check.

* **`GPS_TYPE` = 0** (Disables the GPS module entirely).
* **`ARMING_CHECK`** = Click the "Find" button for this parameter. Uncheck the box for **"GPS"**, but leave the others (like IMU, Baro, Battery) checked for safety.

### 4. Calibration Parameters (You will tune these with the script)

These are the parameters you will actively change based on the results from the `calibrate_optical_flow.py` script.

* **`RNGFND1_SCALING`** (Usually starts at `1.0`. Change this if the drone thinks 1 meter is actually 0.8m or 1.2m).
* **`FLOW_FXSCALER`** (Usually starts at `0`. Tunes your Forward/Backward X-axis distance).
* **`FLOW_FYSCALER`** (Usually starts at `0`. Tunes your Left/Right Y-axis distance).

### 5. Flight Modes

* **`FLTMODE1`** (or whichever switch you use): Must be set to **`GUIDED`**. The Python script will absolutely not work unless the drone is in this mode.

Once you verify these parameters, run the `calibrate_optical_flow.py` script to get your scaler numbers, plug those numbers into Mission Planner, reboot the flight controller, and you will be ready to fly the automated 2x2m grid!
